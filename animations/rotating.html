Rotating Triple Hybrid Accounts

A fixed Algorand account whose authorizing LogicSig rotates on every spend. Each stage’s keys stay hidden behind a hash until it signs, and that same spend hands control to a fresh, never-seen stage—whose signatures must pass Ed25519, Falcon-1024 and hash-based WOTS checks at once.
